Applications

显示面板电子注入修饰层 / 阴极界面层，镱功函数极低（约 2.6eV），溅射沉积超薄镱薄膜，大幅降低阴极与有机发光层之间电子注入势垒，提升电子注入效率，屏幕亮度更高、整机功耗更低；对比镁、钙薄膜化学稳定性更强，能抑制界面反应，延长屏幕使用寿命，适配柔性折叠屏反复弯折工况，减少膜层分层失效。柔性 PI 基底折叠屏、透明 OLED、智能手表穿戴屏专用界面膜，镱薄膜延展性优异，弯折不易开裂；高纯低杂质镀膜无针孔黑点，保障柔性器件良率，耐受高低温车载环境波动。

Used as an electron-injection modification layer or cathode interface layer for display panels. Ytterbium has a very low work function of about 2.6 eV, and sputtered ultrathin ytterbium films can greatly reduce the electron-injection barrier between the cathode and organic emission layer, improving injection efficiency, increasing brightness, and lowering device power consumption. Compared with magnesium and calcium films, ytterbium films have stronger chemical stability, can suppress interface reactions, extend screen lifetime, and reduce delamination under repeated bending in flexible foldable screens. They are suitable for flexible PI-substrate foldable displays, transparent OLEDs, and wearable smartwatch displays, offering good ductility, low cracking risk, low pinhole and black-dot defects, high device yield, and tolerance to vehicle-grade temperature fluctuations.
