Nickel-chromium alloy targets are mainly used to deposit high-precision stable thin-film resistors for thin-film circuits, sensor heating elements, and voltage-divider resistors. They are also used for display-panel electrostatic protection and electromagnetic shielding coatings. Additional applications include optical attenuation films, photothermal absorbing coatings, corrosion-resistant decorative hardware films, and MEMS strain-sensing resistor layers.
