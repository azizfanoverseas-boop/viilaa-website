Applications

主要用于光通信掺铒放大薄膜、2940nm医疗激光晶体基底、红外光电传感镀层。 可搭配其他稀土共蒸合金膜，同时用于半导体过渡层、量子光芯片及光学仪器镀膜、实验室科研试样制备。

Mainly used for erbium-doped optical communication amplification films, 2940 nm medical laser crystal substrates, and infrared optoelectronic sensor coatings. It can also be co-evaporated with other rare earths for alloy films and used for semiconductor transition layers, quantum photonic chips, optical instrument coatings, and laboratory research samples.
