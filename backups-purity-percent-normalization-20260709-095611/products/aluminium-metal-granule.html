Applications

高导电铝膜，主要用于光学反光膜、显示屏电极、PCB 导电线路与半导体器件金属化层。还可制备电磁屏蔽薄膜、锂电池集流体镀层、防潮阻隔膜，也可与其他金属共蒸镀合金薄膜，适配光伏、航天及实验室镀膜研发。

High-conductivity aluminum films are mainly used for optical reflective films, display electrodes, PCB thin-film circuits, and metallization layers for semiconductor devices. They can also be used for electromagnetic shielding films, lithium battery current collector coatings, moisture-barrier films, and co-evaporated alloy films with other metals for photovoltaic, aerospace, and laboratory coating research.
