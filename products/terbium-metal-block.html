Applications

核心制备磁光存储薄膜、绿光发光功能层、永磁改性镀层与红外探测器件涂层。 可与其他稀土共蒸镀多元合金膜，也用于特种光学滤光片、中子屏蔽薄膜、光电与低温磁性科研试样制备。

Mainly used to prepare magneto-optical storage films, green luminescent functional layers, permanent-magnet modification coatings, and infrared detector coatings. It can be co-evaporated with other rare earths to form multi-component alloy films and is also used for specialty optical filters, neutron-shielding films, optoelectronic research, and low-temperature magnetic research samples.
