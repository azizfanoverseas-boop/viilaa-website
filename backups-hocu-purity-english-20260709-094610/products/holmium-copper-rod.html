Applications

钬铜合金凭借优异巨磁热效应，主要用作液氢设备、航天探测器、超导装置的低温磁制冷蓄冷介质。

Holmium-copper alloys, with excellent giant magnetocaloric effects, are mainly used as low-temperature magnetic refrigeration regenerator media for liquid-hydrogen equipment, space probes, and superconducting devices.
